## 📌 Project Overview

This repository highlights public health surveillance, epidemiologic analysis, overdose fatality review activities, and substance use monitoring projects conducted through the CDC Overdose Data to Action (OD2A) program at the Florida Department of Health in Broward County.

My work focuses on overdose surveillance, mortality review, data abstraction, data quality improvement, substance use trend monitoring, and development of data visualizations that support evidence-based overdose prevention efforts throughout Broward County.

Due to public health confidentiality requirements, no patient-level data, surveillance datasets, or internal reports are included in this repository.

# Project 1: Overdose Fatality Reviews (OFR)

## Overview

Overdose Fatality Reviews are multidisciplinary reviews of fatal overdose cases used to identify risk factors, service gaps, missed intervention opportunities, and prevention strategies.

---

## Responsibilities

- Review fatal overdose cases monthly and quarterly
- Abstract information from medical records
- Review EMS encounter data
- Analyze overdose circumstances and contributing factors
- Develop epidemiologic summaries
- Create data visualizations for OFR presentations
- Present findings to multidisciplinary stakeholders

---

## Monthly OFR Activities

- Review approximately 5 fatal overdose cases
- Analyze demographic and risk factor information
- Identify recurring patterns and trends
- Create visual summaries and case presentations

---

## Quarterly OFR Activities

- Review approximately 10 fatal overdose cases
- Conduct expanded analyses across multiple variables
- Evaluate prior EMS encounters
- Assess intervention opportunities
- Develop comprehensive quarterly presentations

---

# Project 2: Component B – Drug Compound Surveillance

## Overview

Component B focuses on monitoring compounds identified in drug products and drug paraphernalia samples collected throughout Broward County.

The project supports surveillance of emerging drug trends and changing substance use patterns.

---

## Responsibilities

- Manage REDCap databases
- Enter and validate laboratory testing results
- Monitor approximately 80 compound-related variables
- Conduct annual surveillance analyses
- Generate monthly reports
- Create data visualizations using R
- Monitor compound co-occurrence patterns
- Identify emerging substance trends
